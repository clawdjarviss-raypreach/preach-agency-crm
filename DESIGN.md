# Preach CRM — Design System

## Brand Reference
Based on Preach Agency website (preachagency.com)

## Color Palette
- **Background:** Dark / near-black (#1a1a1a or #0f0f0f)
- **Surface/Cards:** Dark gray (#2a2a2a or #1e1e1e)
- **Accent (Primary):** Gold/Orange **#f1ae38**
- **Text (Primary):** White (#ffffff)
- **Text (Secondary):** Light gray (#a0a0a0)
- **Borders:** Subtle dark (#333333)

## Design Touches
- Dark, premium feel — matches the agency website
- Gold #f1ae38 for buttons, highlights, active states, badges
- Clean typography, bold headings
- Rounded buttons with gold background + dark text
- Subtle gradients (dark brown → black) for hero sections
- Crown logo element from Preach Agency branding

## Components
- **Buttons:** Gold (#f1ae38) background, dark text, rounded corners
- **Cards:** Dark surface, subtle border or shadow
- **Navigation:** Dark with gold active indicators
- **Status badges:** Gold for positive, muted for neutral
- **Inputs:** Dark background, lighter border, white text

## Previous Theme (deprecated)
- Was: Light warm (bg #f6f5f2, white cards, accent #c4956a)
- Now: Dark premium with gold #f1ae38
